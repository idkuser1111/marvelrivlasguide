● Season 9.5 Meta Notes

Stop feeding.Start carrying.

A practical guide to actually improving at Marvel Rivals — fundamentals that matter more than hero picks, current strong picks by role, and how to climb without tilting.

Vanguard Duelist Strategist
01

Fix these before hero picks matter

Most rank-locked players lose to habits, not hero choice. These fundamentals apply no matter who you play.

A

Positioning over aim

Line of sight decides most fights before a shot is fired. Play around cover, don't peek wide, and never be the first body the enemy team sees.

B

Track cooldowns, not just health

Watch when the enemy tank's shield or the healer's cleanse is on cooldown. That window is when your team should commit.

C

Ultimate economy

Don't blow your ult to get one pick. Bank it, communicate it, and use it to answer the enemy's ultimate or open a fight your team can win.

D

Target priority

Default to the backline unless someone is diving your healers. A dead Strategist swings a fight harder than a dead tank.

E

Ping, don't type

Fast, minimal comms win fights. Call ults and dive threats the second you see them — by the time you've typed it, the window's gone.

F

Review your own deaths

After a loss, check the killcam on your first 2–3 deaths each match. A pattern (overextending, no escape ability up) will show up fast.

Don't blindly copy tier lists

S-tier at Diamond+ isn't always S-tier at Gold or Platinum. Below Diamond, coordination matters less than raw mechanical comfort — pick heroes you can execute consistently over heroes that are "meta" on paper.

03

How to actually climb

Rank comes from consistency, not one great game. Here's the order to work on things.

Narrow your hero pool to 2–3 per role

You don't need to know all 51+ heroes. Master a couple of Vanguards, Duelists, and Strategists so you can always counter-pick without playing something unfamiliar.

Learn to counter-pick, not just main

If the enemy runs a dive comp, swap into a hero with disengage or burst peel. Adapting your pick to the enemy team wins more games than one-tricking.

Play team fights around cooldown windows

Group engages should happen when the enemy's key defensive cooldowns (shields, cleanses, escapes) are down — not just whenever your team happens to be near each other.

Warm up aim separately from ranked

Spend 10–15 minutes in the practice range or a quick match before queuing competitive. Cold-aim ranked games are where most avoidable losses happen.

Track your losses, not your wins

Keep a mental (or literal) note of the last three losing streaks. If the same mistake shows up twice, that's the thing to fix next session — not hero picks.
